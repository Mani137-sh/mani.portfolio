About Me

I am a Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, specializing in Integrated Circuits and Systems. My research focuses on ASIC Design, RTL Design, FPGA Prototyping, Digital VLSI, and Navigation Receiver Architectures. I enjoy translating complex algorithms into efficient hardware, from Verilog RTL development and FPGA validation to ASIC implementation using Cadence Genus and Innovus.

Featured Research

Design of Acquisition Module for the NavIC Receiver Chip

Designed and implemented an ASIC-oriented GPS/NavIC acquisition architecture using Verilog RTL. The work includes PRN generation, carrier generation using a fixed-point NCO, multi-bin Doppler search, MAC-based correlation, FPGA prototyping on PYNQ-Z2 and complete ASIC implementation using Cadence Genus and Innovus.
