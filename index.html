Who am I?

I am a Ph.D. Research Scholar at the Department of Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. My research focuses on designing high-performance and low-power hardware architectures for GPS/NavIC acquisition receivers targeting ASIC implementation. I have industrial experience as an RTL Design Engineer at Chipspirit Technologies where I worked on FPGA based Encryption and Decryption projects. My expertise spans RTL Design, FPGA Prototyping, Physical Design, ASIC Implementation, Verification and Hardware Validation.
