About Me

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, specializing in Integrated Circuits and Systems. My research focuses on ASIC Design, RTL Design, FPGA Prototyping, Digital VLSI, and Navigation Receiver Architectures. I enjoy transforming algorithms into efficient hardware implementations, from Verilog RTL development and FPGA validation to physical design using Cadence tools.
